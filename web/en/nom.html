How to buy

  1. Install UZARO and sign in with your Telegram account.
  2. Top up your balance in the app: Profile → UZARO username → “Top up balance”. Transfer to the card and upload the receipt photo; an admin approves it.
  3. Check the name on this page and tap “Buy in the Telegram bot”.
  4. In the bot, see the price and tap the buy command — the name is yours at once. You can also buy it right in the app.

The name is tied to your UZARO account and replaces your current UZARO username. The price is taken from your balance.
