You decide who sees what

Privacy controls live in the side menu and turn on with one tap.

  • Ghost mode

    Your online status, read receipts and “typing…” are not sent. Watch stories without being noticed.

  • Deleted messages stay

    When someone deletes or edits a message, the original stays on your device. The archive is never sent to a server.

  • PIN lock and hidden chats

    Lock the app with a 6-digit PIN. While the lock is on, chats are hidden from the app switcher and from screenshots.

  • Warnings for risky files and links

    You get a warning before opening program files such as .apk and .exe, or suspicious links. It is not an antivirus, so check where a file came from.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find an answer? Read the guide. Open the guide

Does it work with my Telegram account?

Yes. UZARO works through Telegram’s open API (TDLib): the same number, chats and contacts. UZARO is not an official Telegram app.

Can UZARO see my messages?

No. Messages, calls and files go straight to Telegram’s servers. The UZARO server only stores business-section data: shops, products and ads.

Is the app paid?

No. Chats, calls, Ghost mode and your first 2 shops are free. Only ads are paid, and the app shows the price before you pay.

Where are deleted messages kept?

Only on your phone. They are not sent to the UZARO server or anywhere else.

How many numbers can I add?

Up to 12. Switch between them with one tap.

Is installing the APK safe?

The easiest way is Google Play: the app is checked there and updates itself. Get the APK only from uzaromessenger.uz, and don’t install “UZARO” files shared in other channels.

Is there a version for iPhone or computers?

The Windows 10 and 11 version is ready to download from this site. The iPhone version is on the App Store.
