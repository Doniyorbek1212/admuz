How it works

  1. Confirm your Telegram account in the UZARO app: Profile → ⋮ → Gifts. You do this once.
  2. On this page tap “Sign in with Telegram”, then “Start” in the bot.
  3. Pick an amount and pay in Payme. The money reaches your wallet right after payment.

In @UzaroPay_bot itself you can also top up with Telegram Stars, TON or a card.
